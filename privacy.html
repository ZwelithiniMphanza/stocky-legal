About this policy

This policy describes how Stocky handles information when you manage inventory, purchases, sales, services, and staff on an Android device. Stocky is provided by Zwelithini Mphanza, publishing from Zambia. For support or privacy inquiries, email zwelitech@gmail.com.

Business records and local accounts

Stocky stores your business name, currency, product and service details, quantities, costs, sales, purchase records, staff usernames, roles, account status, and salted password hashes in a local SQLite database. Transaction history identifies the staff account that recorded a transaction. Session credentials use Android secure storage. Local admin and staff accounts do not create an online Stocky account. Administrators can access business records and manage staff access. Stocky does not upload this business database to a developer-operated server.

Backups and security

When you export a backup, Stocky writes a complete business database copy, including account password hashes, to the destination you choose. Exported files are not password-encrypted. Keep them private and protect your device with a screen lock. Import replaces the local business database after confirmation, creates a local recovery copy first, and signs users out. Recovery copies also contain business and account data. Stocky does not currently connect to Google Drive. If you place an exported file in a cloud folder or share it, that destination's policies apply. The SQLite database is protected by Android's app storage controls but is not separately encrypted by Stocky.

Advertising, when enabled

Ad-supported releases use Google AdMob for banner advertisements. Google's SDK may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Stocky requests non-personalized ads; these can still involve data processing. Applicable consent is requested before ads are enabled. Ad privacy choices are available in More when Google's consent system requires them. Premium users are not shown ads. Ads are requested only in builds where advertising is enabled. Reading this policy is not consent to advertising.

Premium purchases, when enabled

Google Play processes Premium payments. Stocky uses RevenueCat to verify purchases and restore ad-free access. RevenueCat processes an installation-associated app user identifier, purchase history and tokens, entitlement status, and relevant app, device, platform, and transaction metadata. It can receive this information when its configured SDK checks purchase status, even before a new purchase. Stocky does not receive your payment-card details and does not send local usernames, passwords, inventory records, or backup files to RevenueCat through this integration. Purchasing is available only in builds where it is enabled; the Premium screen indicates availability.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until the app's stored data is cleared, the app is uninstalled, or records are replaced by importing a backup. Archiving products and disabling staff preserve transaction history; they do not delete those records. An authorized device owner can remove local app data through Android Settings > Apps > Stocky > Storage > Clear storage (wording varies), or uninstall Stocky. This permanently removes the local business database; back up anything you need first. Separately delete exported backups and copies shared or stored elsewhere. Clearing Stocky does not delete Google Play purchase records or RevenueCat records, refund a purchase, or withdraw previously submitted support messages. Contact the publisher for requests concerning developer-controlled support or purchase-service data. Payment providers may retain records for legal, accounting, security, or dispute purposes. Their retention rules also apply.

Support, rights, and international processing

If you contact support, the publisher receives the information you choose to send and uses it to answer your request. Do not send passwords or complete business backups unless a support process specifically requires and explains a secure transfer. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact the publisher to make a request. Google and RevenueCat may process information outside your country under their applicable safeguards. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to resolve the inquiry, follow up on related issues, and meet applicable legal or dispute-handling obligations, then deleted when no longer needed. Send privacy or deletion requests to zwelitech@gmail.com, describing the data concerned. We may request information reasonably needed to verify the request. Do not include passwords or full payment-card details.

Audience and changes

Stocky is intended for business operators and authorized staff, not directed to children. Material changes to these practices will be reflected in an updated policy and any additional notice or consent required by law. Consult Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/ for their practices.
